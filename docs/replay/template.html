Each line is one controller braking from 270 m/s to 1 m/s (log scales). The dashed line is the terminal gate, 12 m above the arms.

Simulation, not SpaceX data. Vehicle numbers are public estimates (Raptor 2: 2.26 MN, 40–100% throttle; Super Heavy empty ~275 t). The simulator tracks the booster's centre of mass; the replay draws the body leaning partway into the thrust and the engines gimballing the rest (as a real booster does), with the tower, arms and booster drawn from public photos at roughly the right scale. Engines: 13 at ignition, the 3 centre engines from 3 s.

Catch tolerance: within 1 m of the catch point, under 1.5 m/s down, under 0.5 m/s sideways, tilt under 3°. A booster that cannot be caught diverts offshore; coming down more than 40 m from the tower counts as a safe miss.
