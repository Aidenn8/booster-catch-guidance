Simulation, not SpaceX data. Vehicle numbers are public estimates (Raptor 2: 2.26 MN, 40–100% throttle; Super Heavy empty ~275 t); the booster is a point mass whose thrust direction stands in for its attitude, and the tower, arms and booster are drawn from public photos at roughly the right scale. Engines: 13 at ignition, the 3 centre engines from 3 s. The world adds drag, gusty wind, thrust and mass errors, sensor noise and engine failures.

Catch tolerance: within 1 m of the catch point, under 1.5 m/s down, under 0.5 m/s sideways, tilt under 3°. A booster that cannot be caught diverts offshore; coming down more than 40 m from the tower counts as a safe miss.
